Content-aware issue sign-off

Know exactly which issue content was approved.

Signlight adds a simple traffic-light sign-off to Jira issues. Green means the relevant content is signed. Yellow means it changed. Red means there is no active sign-off.

The traffic-light workflow

Only meaningful content changes the light.

Signlight fingerprints the summary, description and attachment set. Routine comments and status changes do not invalidate an otherwise current sign-off.

Details on demand

Timestamp and scope

Open the compact detail to see when and what was signed.

Green

Issue content signed

The active sign-off matches the issue's relevant content.

Red

Unsigned or revoked

No active sign-off exists for the issue.

Small by design

Visible accountability without approval theatre.

Signlight is for lightweight internal acknowledgement and approval. It is not a regulated or legally binding e-signature product.

Atlassian identity

People sign with the account already logged into Jira.

Content aware

Summary, description and attachments define the signed state.

Revocable

Only the active signer can revoke, with a bounded audit event.

No content copy

Signlight stores a fingerprint and identifiers—not issue content.

Hosted on Forge

Your sign-off flow stays inside Atlassian.

The app runs on Atlassian Forge, uses Forge app storage and declares no external network egress. There is no separate Signlight account, password or external dashboard.
